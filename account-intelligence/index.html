How the score works

Partner Fit Score is a weighted blend of six factors read from the 10-K: Financial Health (25%), Growth Momentum (20%), Marketing Intensity — SG&A and advertising spend as a demand signal (18%), Solvency & Liquidity (15%), Risk Profile — inverted from the density and severity of risk-factor and going-concern language (12%), and Strategic Fit (10%). ≥70 → Pursue, 50–69 → Watch, <50 → Avoid.
